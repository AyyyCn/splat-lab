What to look for
  1. Let it settle, then hold the compare button. The path traced frame has light bouncing off the ground into the shadow side of rocks, contact darkening where things touch, and shadows that soften with distance. Realtime has a shadow map and sky light only.
  2. Drag the sun angle. Every shadow, bounce and reflection follows, because nothing is baked.
  3. Switch skies. Same scans, different real light. Overcast flatters scans; low sun is the hard test.
  4. Find where it breaks. Orbit and watch the far hills: they're a photo at infinity, so they never shift against the rocks. Past about 30 m the scanned ground fades into the photo's ground, hidden behind a line of boulders. Nothing moves in the wind.
