What to look for
  1. Move the lamp. The mesh's shading and shadow follow it. The room's own light stays put, because it's baked into the splat.
  2. Turn on fake-lighting. Splats near the lamp brighten. It's a colour edit, not light, so nothing blocks it: it glows through furniture.
  3. Switch the environment. "Captured from the splat room" makes the chrome ball reflect the actual room, and it's dark behind you because this splat is only half a room. "Studio" is brighter but looks pasted in.
  4. Watch the shadow. It lands only on the invisible floor plane, never on the walls or furniture.
